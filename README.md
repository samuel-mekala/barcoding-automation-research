## 📌 Abstract

This research investigates the **impact of barcoding and automation technologies on the operational performance** of organisations — specifically within the pharmaceutical sector.

Using a **quantitative survey methodology** (n=250 pharmaceutical industry professionals in Hyderabad), and **Structural Equation Modelling (SEM)** via SPSS AMOS 21, we tested whether barcoding and automation positively affect operational performance.

**Both hypotheses were rejected** — meaning barcoding (β=0.542) and automation (β=0.779) **do** significantly improve operational performance.

## 📊 Key Findings

### Hypotheses Tested

**H01:** Barcoding has no positive impact on operational performance  
**H02:** Automation has no positive impact on operational performance

### Results

| Path | Estimate (β) | C.R. | P-value | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OPP ← Barcoding | **0.542** | 8.161 | 0.000 | ✅ H01 Rejected (significant) |
| OPP ← Automation | **0.779** | 9.897 | 0.000 | ✅ H02 Rejected (significant) |

> Both barcoding and automation significantly improve operational performance (p < 0.001).

## 🧩 Operational Performance Constructs Measured

**Barcoding → affects:**
- Asset Tracking
- Invoicing (β=0.808)
- Inventory Management (β=0.607)

**Automation → affects:**
- Measure Right Metrics
- Internet of Things (β=0.837)
- Wrapping Up / Process Closure (β=1.035)

**Operational Performance → outputs:**
- Monitoring
- Scorecard (β=0.446)
- Preference Control (β=0.151)
- Resource Optimization (β=0.980)
